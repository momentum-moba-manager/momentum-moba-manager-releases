What you can do

  • ✅ Full match simulation: laning, ganks, sieges, objectives and team fights on a live map
  • ✅ Watch matches live at 1x, 2x or 4x with a gold difference chart and end-of-game stats, and reopen any played match game by game
  • ✅ Damage types and tank durability that shape every fight — stack too much of one damage type and pay for it, pick anti-tank to cut through a tough frontline
  • ✅ Interactive draft with ban/pick and Fearless Draft across Bo1, Bo3 and Bo5, against CPU teams that draft around their players' comfort picks, follow a game plan and answer your picks with counters, while your staff points out what your composition still needs
  • ✅ Free pick order with flex picks: take champions in any role order, trade them between your players until the draft ends, and read the opponent's hidden roles from their champions, as they read yours
  • ✅ Lane and jungle matchups that matter: a counter-pick wins trades, duels and ganks, and engage, disengage and poke comps beat one another rock-paper-scissors style over the whole match
  • ✅ Six-dimension team strategy (split push, siege, objective control, teamfight, pick, scaling)
  • ✅ Champion meta that shifts over time, with whole classes of champions rising or falling together, and patch notes with a tier list, flex picks and every champion's grade per role
  • ✅ Match ratings with Player of the Game / Player of the Series and per-athlete career stats
  • ✅ Athlete growth, decline, retirement and procedural generation of new pros, with unsigned free agents eventually leaving the scene
  • ✅ Full competitive calendar: regular season, playoffs, MSI, Regional Finals and Worlds (Swiss Stage included)
  • ✅ Club finances: income/expenses, sponsors and prize money
  • ✅ Contract expiry, free agency, wage budget and transfer negotiations
  • ✅ The full Tier 1 world: every major region, league, club, coach and athlete
  • ✅ Staff notes during the draft that point out what your comp still needs, with one click to see the champions that answer it
  • ✅ Play in English or Italian, switchable at any time from the game options
  • ✅ Automatic updates: the game asks at startup and installs the new version for you

What you can't still do (in roadmap)

  • ❌ Customize strategy ad-hoc for each individual athlete based on their role
  • ❌ Player injuries and burnout
  • ❌ Firing a coach or a coaching job market
  • ❌ A scouting network to discover and sign new talent
  • ❌ Club objectives, board pressure and season-by-season history
  • ❌ Staff beyond the head coach: analysts, assistant coaches
  • ❌ Personal relationships, locker-room dynamics and one-on-one talks with athletes
  • ❌ Match rating affecting morale or growth, and split/season MVP rankings
  • ❌ A presented match viewer beyond text commentary

Install

  1. Download with the button above. It always gets the newest build.
  2. Run Momentum-MOBA-Manager-Setup-x64.exe. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights.
  3. That's it. From v0.0.4 on, the game asks on startup to install each new version for you.
"Windows protected your PC"? The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognize it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Report a bug

  1. Open the game folder. In a career, click the gear icon, then the Game folder button.
  2. Attach the log from the logs folder: game.log is the current or last session. The game starts a new log every time it opens, so if you restarted after the bug, attach the newest game_<date>.log instead.
  3. Attach your save from the saves folder if the bug happened in a career, so it can be reproduced from the same point.
  4. Say what you did right before it happened, and what you expected instead.
The game doesn't start? Paste %APPDATA%\com.mmm.moba-manager into the File Explorer address bar to reach the same folder.

Usage statistics

To know how many people install and actually play the alpha, the game sends a few anonymous counts to Aptabase, a privacy-first analytics service. Only when it starts:

  • What's sent: that the game was installed or started, the game version, your operating system and its version, your system language and the WebView version.
  • What isn't: no name, account or ID that follows you, and nothing about your careers, saves or settings.
  • What stays on your PC: telemetry.json in the game folder, which only remembers whether this install, week and month were already counted.
Don't want to send them? Turn off Anonymous usage statistics in the settings, from the Main Menu or the gear icon in a career. Nothing is sent from then on.
