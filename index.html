The game

What you do as head coach
01

Draft

Ban and pick against teams that draft around their players, with Fearless series and your staff's advice.

02

Watch it play out

Every lane, gank, objective and teamfight is simulated, and you follow the match live.

03

Build the roster

Sign free agents, negotiate contracts and watch young players grow while veterans fade.

04

Run the club

Wages, sponsors and prize money: keep the accounts healthy or pay for it.

05

A living world

Every major league plays its season around you, all the way to MSI and Worlds.

06

A shifting meta

Patches move champions up and down the tier list, so last split's best picks may not work.

Already in the game

  • Every Tier 1 region, league, club and player, with a full season calendar up to Worlds
  • Interactive draft with Fearless series, counters, flex picks and staff notes
  • Live matches with commentary, a post-game review and ratings for every player
  • Contracts, free agency, early terminations, sponsors and club finances
  • Players who grow, retire and are replaced by new talent
  • Play in English or Italian, with automatic updates

Not in the game yet

  • Injuries and burnout
  • Getting fired and looking for a new coaching job
  • Club objectives and pressure from the board
  • Assistant coaches, analysts and scouts
  • Morale, locker-room dynamics and one-on-one talks with players

Help

Installing and updating
  1. Download with the button above: it always gets the newest version.
  2. Run Momentum-MOBA-Manager-Setup-x64.exe. It installs for your user only, no administrator rights needed.
  3. That's it. When a new version comes out, the game offers to install it at startup.

This is an early build: saves from one version may not load in the next.

Reporting a bug
  1. Open the game folder: in a career, click the gear icon, then Game folder.
  2. Attach the log from the logs folder: game.log is the last session. If you restarted the game after the bug, attach the newest game_<date>.log instead.
  3. Attach your save from the saves folder, so the bug can be replayed from the same point.
  4. Tell what you did right before it happened, and what you expected.

If the game doesn't start, paste %APPDATA%\com.mmm.moba-manager into the File Explorer address bar to reach the same folder.

Anonymous usage statistics

When it starts, the game sends Aptabase a few anonymous counts, to know how many people install and play the alpha.

  • Sent: that the game was installed or started, its version, your operating system, your system language and the WebView version.
  • Never sent: your name, an account or an ID that follows you, or anything about your careers, saves and settings.
  • Kept on your PC: telemetry.json in the game folder, which only remembers what was already counted.

To stop it, turn off Anonymous usage statistics in the settings.
