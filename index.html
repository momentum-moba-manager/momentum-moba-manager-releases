What you can do

  • ✅ Full match simulation: laning, ganks, sieges, objectives and team fights on a live map
  • ✅ Damage types and tank durability that shape every fight — stack too much of one damage type and pay for it, pick anti-tank to cut through a tough frontline
  • ✅ Interactive draft with ban/pick and Fearless Draft across Bo1, Bo3 and Bo5
  • ✅ Six-dimension team strategy (split push, siege, objective control, teamfight, pick, scaling)
  • ✅ Champion meta that shifts over time, with patch notes and a tier list
  • ✅ Match ratings with Player of the Game / Player of the Series and per-athlete career stats
  • ✅ Athlete growth, decline, retirement and procedural generation of new pros
  • ✅ Full competitive calendar: regular season, playoffs, MSI, Regional Finals and Worlds (Swiss Stage included)
  • ✅ Club finances: income/expenses, sponsors and prize money
  • ✅ Contract expiry, free agency, wage budget and transfer negotiations
  • ✅ The full Tier 1 world: every major region, league, club, coach and athlete

What you can't still do (in roadmap)

  • ❌ Customize strategy ad-hoc for each individual athlete based on their role
  • ❌ Player injuries and burnout
  • ❌ Firing a coach or a coaching job market
  • ❌ A scouting network to discover and sign new talent
  • ❌ Club objectives, board pressure and season-by-season history
  • ❌ Staff beyond the head coach: analysts, assistant coaches
  • ❌ Personal relationships, locker-room dynamics and one-on-one talks with athletes
  • ❌ Match rating affecting morale or growth, and split/season MVP rankings
  • ❌ A presented match viewer beyond text commentary

Changelog

v0.0.2 2026-09-23

  • Damage types and tank durability now matter: a team that deals only one kind of damage struggles against a durable frontline, anti-tank champions shine against tanks, and the CPU drafts with this in mind.
  • Champions now show a colored ring for their damage type (physical, magic, or split for hybrid) wherever they appear: draft, champion pool, tier list, and the balance patch notes email.
  • Champion fit is fairer: an athlete's affinity with a champion now adds to its value instead of multiplying it.
  • Each career has a seed, so the same seed replays the same world.
  • Settings open as a modal and confirm when your changes are saved.
  • Finances moved from Home to the Society tab.
  • The Tactics screen always shows the blue side on the left and red on the right.
  • Fixed the inbox not being kept correctly between games, and the Back button can no longer take you out of a running career.
  • The game window now has a minimum size of 1280x720.

v0.0.1 2026-09-21

  • The full career loop: create a coach, pick a club, play drafts and matches, run seasons with international events.
  • Free agency, contracts, sponsors and club finances.
  • The game now checks for a newer build on startup.

Install

  1. Download with the button above. It always gets the newest build.
  2. Run Momentum-MOBA-Manager-Setup-x64.exe. It installs for your user only and doesn't need administrator rights.
  3. That's it. The game tells you on startup when a newer build is out.
"Windows protected your PC"? The installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen doesn't recognize it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

This is an early, private build shared with a few friends for feedback. Expect rough edges, and saves from one version may not load in the next.
